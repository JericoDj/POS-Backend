# Fixed POS Pricing Structure (Subscription)

## 🧪 Free — “Trial but usable”
**Price:** $0 / month

**Description:** Start selling easily with simple essential POS tools.

**Goal:** Let micro sellers actually run a tiny business and trust the system

**Limits:**
- 100 orders / month
- 50 products
- 1 device
- Basic receipt
- Daily sales report only
- No backups
- No staff accounts

**💡 Why this works:**
They can operate a real store for 2–3 weeks → attachment happens → upgrade becomes natural.

---

## 🟢 Starter — For small real stores
**Price:** $19 / month

**Description:** Essential tools for small businesses ready to grow without limits.

**Goal:** Remove fear. Remove tiny limits.

**Limits/Features:**
- Unlimited orders
- 500 products
- 2 devices
- Basic reports
- Inventory tracking
- Expenses
- Backup (manual)
- **Excluded:** No analytics, no staff permissions, no advanced features.

**💡 Why this works:**
Now $19 feels fair because business can operate fully.

---

## 🔵 Pro — Main plan (most customers)
**Price:** $59 / month

**Description:** Advanced features for bustling shops, cafes, and boutiques.

**Target:** café, milk tea, boutique, minimart

**Features:**
- Unlimited orders
- Unlimited products
- 5 staff accounts
- Advanced reports
- Profit analytics
- Supplier management
- Stock alerts
- Customer list
- Automatic backups
- Bluetooth printing
- Discounts & promos

**💡 Why this works:**
This becomes your real revenue engine.

---

## 🟣 Business — Serious business
**Price:** $119 / month

**Description:** Complete management for high-volume restaurants and serious retail businesses.

**Target:** restaurants & busy stores

**Features:**
- Everything in Pro
- Unlimited staff
- Multi-device unlimited
- Kitchen display / order queue
- Role permissions
- Audit logs
- Branch support (1 main + 1 sub)
- Cloud sync real-time

---

## 🟡 Enterprise — Chains
**Price:** $199 / month

**Description:** Scalable solutions for multi-location chains and franchises.

**Target:** Chains

**Features:**
- Multi-branch unlimited
- Central dashboard
- Remote monitoring
- Priority support
- Data export API
- White-label
